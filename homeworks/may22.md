## 一

1. $y = \ln {x} - 2$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \ln {x} \\
    &\therefore x > 0\\
    &x \in (0, \infty)
\end{align*}
$$

2. $y = \arcsin {2x} - 1$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \arcsin {2x} \\
    &\therefore 2x \in [-1, 1] \\
    &x \in [-\frac {1} {2}, \frac {1} {2}]
\end{align*}
$$

3. $y = \arccos {\frac {x} {3}}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \arccos {\frac {x} {3}} \\
    &\therefore \frac {x} {3} \in [-1, 1] \\
    &x \in [-3, 3]
\end{align*}
$$

4. $y = \frac {1} {\ln {x} + 1}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \ln {x} \\
    &\therefore x \in (0, \infty) \\
    &\because \frac {1} {\ln {x} + 1} \\
    &\therefore \ln {x} + 1 \neq 0 \\
    &\therefore x \neq {e}^{-1} \\
    &x \in (0, {e}^{-1}) \cup ({e}^{-1}, \infty)
\end{align*}
$$

5. $y = \sqrt {\ln {x}}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \ln {x} \\
    &\therefore x \in (0, \infty) \\
    &\because \sqrt {\ln {x}} \\
    &\therefore \ln {x} \geq 0 \\
    &\therefore x \in [1, \infty) \\
    &x \in [1, \infty)
\end{align*}
$$

6. $y = \ln {4} - x + \frac {1} {x - 1}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \frac {1} {x - 1} \\
    &\therefore x - 1 \neq 0 \\
    &\therefore x\neq 1 \\
    &x \in (-\infty, 1) \cup (1, \infty)
\end{align*}
$$

7. $y = \arcsin {\frac {x - 1} {2}} + \sqrt {3 - x}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \sqrt {3 - x} \\
    &\therefore 3 - x \geq 0 \\
    &\therefore x \leq 3 \\
    &\because \arcsin {\frac {x - 1} {2}} \\
    &\therefore \frac {x - 1} {2} \in [-1, 1] \\
    &\therefore x \in [-1, 3] \\
    &x \in [-1, 3]
\end{align*}
$$

8. $y = \frac {\arccos {2x}} {\ln {x} - 1}$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &\because \ln {x} - 1 \neq 0 \\
    &\therefore x \neq e \\
    &\because \arccos {2x} \\
    &\therefore 2x \in [-1, 1] \\
    &\therefore \in [-\frac {1} {2}, \frac {1} {2}] \\
    &x \in [-\frac {1} {2}, \frac {1} {2}]
\end{align*}
$$

> \end {align*}
> $$

## 二

1. domain of $f(x)$ is $[1, 5]$, ask domain of $f(x + 2)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &1 \leq x \leq 5 \\
    &1 \leq x + 2 \leq 5 \\
    &-1 \leq x \leq 3 \\
    &x \in [-1, 3]
\end{align*}
$$

2. domain of $f(x - 1)$ is $[2, 4]$, ask domain of $f(x)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &2 \leq x \leq 4 \\
    &2 - 1 \leq x - 1 \leq 4 - 1 \\
    &x \in [1, 3]
\end{align*}
$$

3. domain of $f(2x + 1)$ is $[0, 3]$, ask domain of $f(x)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &0 \leq x \leq 3 \\
    &0 \leq 2x \leq 6 \\
    &1 \leq 2x + 1 \leq 7 \\
    &x \in [1, 7]
\end{align*}
$$

4. domain of $f(x)$ is $[-2, 3]$, ask domain of $f(2x - 1)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &-2 \leq x \leq 3 \\
    &-2 \leq 2x - 1 \leq 3 \\
    &-\frac {1} {2} \leq x \leq 2 \\
    &x \in [-\frac {1} {2}, 2]
\end{align*}
$$

5. domain of $f(x + 3)$ is $[-4, 2]$, ask domain of $f(2x - 1)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &-4 \leq x \leq 2 \\
    &-4 + 3 \leq x + 3 \leq 2 + 3 \\
    &-1 \leq x + 3 \leq 5 \\
    &-1 \leq 2x - 1 \leq 5 \\
    &0 \leq x \leq 3 \\
    &x \in [0, 3]
\end{align*}
$$

6. domain of $f(3x - 2)$ is $[1, 3]$, ask domain of $f(x + 1)$

$$
\begin{align*}
    &1 \leq x \leq 3 \\
    &1 \times 3 \leq x \times 3 \leq 3 \times 3 \\
    &3 - 2 \leq 3x - 2 \leq 9 - 2 \\
    &1 \leq 3x - 2 \leq 7 \\
    &1 \leq x + 1 \leq 7 \\
    &0 \leq x \leq 6 \\
    &x \in [0, 6]
\end{align*}
$$
