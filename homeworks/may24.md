## 二

1. $f(x) = 2x + 1$, $g(x) = x - 3$, ask $f[g(x)]$

$$
    \begin{align*}
        f[g(x)] &= 2(x - 3) + 1\\
            &= 2x - 6 + 1\\
            &= 2x - 5
    \end{align*}
$$

2. $f(x) = {x}^{2} - 2x$, $g(x) = 2x$, ask $f[g(x)]$

$$
    \begin{align*}
        f[g(x)] &= {(2x)}^{2} - 2(2x)\\
            &= 4{x}^{2} - 4x
    \end{align*}
$$

3. $f(x) = \frac {1} {x}$, $g(x) = x + 1$, ask $f[g(x)]$

$$
    \begin{align*}
        f[g(x)] &= \frac {1} {x + 1}
    \end{align*}
$$

4. $f(x) = {x}^{2} + 1$, $g(x) = \frac {1} {x - 1}$, ask $f[g(x)]$

$$
    \begin{align*}
        f[g(x)] &= {(\frac {1} {x - 1})}^{2} + 1\\
            &= \frac {1} {{x}^{2} - 2x + 1} + 1\\
            &= \frac {{x}^{2} - 2x + 1 + 1} {{x}^{2} - 2x + 1}\\
            &= \frac {{x}^{2} -2x + 2} {{x}^{2} - 2x + 1}\\ 
    \end{align*}
$$

5. $f(x) = 2{x}^{2} - x + 3$, $g(x) = x + 2$, ask $f[g(x)]$

$$
    \begin{align*}
        f[g(x)] &= 2{(x + 2)}^{2} - (x + 2) + 3\\
            &= 2({x}^{2} + 4x + 4) - (x + 2) + 3\\
            &= 2{x}^{2} + 8x + 8 - x - 2 + 3\\
            &= 2{x}^{2} + 7x + 9
    \end{align*}
$$
